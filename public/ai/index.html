Fantasy Game Classes

Warrior / Fighter

Primary Stats: Strength, Endurance

Abilities: Physical combat, Weapon expertise, High defense

Role: Tank / Damage Dealer

Mage / Wizard

Primary Stats: Intelligence, Mana

Abilities: Spells, Arcane magic, High damage potential

Role: Damage Dealer (Ranged)

Cleric / Priest

Primary Stats: Wisdom, Mana

Abilities: Healing, Buffs, Divine spells

Role: Healer / Support

Rogue / Thief

Primary Stats: Agility, Dexterity

Abilities: Stealth, Backstabbing, Trap disarming

Role: Damage Dealer (Stealth)

Ranger

Primary Stats: Dexterity, Endurance

Abilities: Archery, Beast summoning, Nature magic

Role: Ranged DPS / Utility

Paladin

Primary Stats: Strength, Wisdom

Abilities: Healing, Damage mitigation, Holy magic

Role: Tank / Support

Barbarian

Primary Stats: Strength, Endurance

Abilities: Rage, High physical damage, Tanking

Role: Tank / DPS

Bard

Primary Stats: Charisma, Intelligence

Abilities: Music magic, Buffs, Debuffs

Role: Support / Crowd Control

Druid

Primary Stats: Wisdom, Intelligence

Abilities: Shape-shifting, Nature magic, Healing

Role: Healer / Hybrid DPS

Necromancer

Primary Stats: Intelligence, Mana

Abilities: Summon undead, Death magic, Curse spells

Role: DPS / Utility
